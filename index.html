About Me

I'm KIPCHIRCHIR TOO, a passionate front-end developer based in Nairobi, Kenya, with a strong eye for detail and a love for turning ideas into interactive, user-friendly websites. With a Diploma in Software Engineering from Moringa School and certifications from FreeCodeCamp, HubSpot Academy, and Simplilearn, I’ve built a solid foundation in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React. Beyond code, I bring over 3 years of hands-on experience in virtual assistance, SEO content writing, academic research, and chat moderation—skills that have sharpened my problem-solving, communication, and multitasking abilities. Currently pursuing a Bachelor of Law at the University of Nairobi, I combine analytical thinking with creative tech skills to deliver clean, responsive, and accessible web experiences. I thrive on continuous learning, whether it's diving into new frameworks or refining my GitHub and portfolio projects.
